# 公司私信话术 SOP（飞书《🌡️ 私信话术》，赖致成，2026-10-04 修订）

来源：https://bannimei.feishu.cn/wiki/DqF6w3HVci5h2FkbdIkcf2u3nyf
2026-10-22 用户指定为 FB/IG 私信执行的正式口径，取代此前自拟的"干货答疑式"私信稿。

## 用途与定位
INS 与 Facebook 私信转 WhatsApp。运营任务：礼貌回应、说明机构在上海、邀请客户留带国家区号的 WhatsApp 号码，咨询团队后续接手。**不在平台私信内推荐项目、做诊断、承诺效果。**

## 使用重点
- 先感谢客户联系，再询问 WhatsApp 号码
- 费用只说需结合个人情况进一步沟通
- 号码必须带国家区号
- 不推荐治疗方案、不诊断、不承诺效果
- 客户不愿给号码时不反复催促

## 标准回复（咨询项目/效果/费用时）
Hi [Name], thank you for reaching out to us.
We're based in Shanghai. The exact cost depends on your individual situation and the procedure involved.
If you're comfortable sharing it, could you send us your WhatsApp number, including the country code? Our consultation team will contact you directly to learn more about your concerns and provide further information.
（不知姓名时删 [Name]）

## 简短回复（只有 Hi / Price? 时）
Hi, thank you for contacting us. Could you share your WhatsApp number with the country code? Our consultation team will contact you directly and provide further information.

先确认问题版：
Hi, thank you for reaching out. Which treatment or concern would you like to learn more about?
客户回复后：
Thank you for sharing. Could you send us your WhatsApp number, including the country code? Our consultation team will contact you directly.

## 收到号码后
Thank you, [Name]. I've received your WhatsApp number.
Our consultation team will contact you shortly. Please look out for a message from our team.
Best,
[Your name]
（不能保证立即联系时把 shortly 改成明确时间，如 within 24 hours）

## 缺少国家区号
Thank you. Could you also share the country code for this number? This will help our team contact you correctly on WhatsApp.
（禁止自行猜测区号）

## 客户不愿提供号码
No problem. You're welcome to continue the conversation here. If you decide to contact us on WhatsApp later, we'll be happy to assist you.
（有链接时可附：No problem. You can contact us through WhatsApp whenever it's convenient for you: [WhatsApp link]。我方确认链接：https://drqh.jianjianmeili.com/vMPEX84o）

## 发送前检查
- 姓名正确；WhatsApp 拼写正确；已要求国家区号
- 没误发上一位客户的姓名/信息
- 用的是公司确认的 WhatsApp 链接
- 没有承诺具体效果、诊断或固定价格
