Having Plastic Surgery Soon?
Let's Talk Protein Drinks.
What I recommend to my recovery patients🥤
One thing I notice with my patients is how easy it is to overlook nutrition when you're busy preparing for surgery.
And after surgery? Your appetite may not be great, especially during those first few days.
But your body still needs adequate protein to support tissue repair and healing.
That's why I often discuss convenient nutritional options with my clients.
🥤 Fairlife Core Power & Quest: Easy ways to supplement daily protein intake when meals aren't providing enough.
🥤 Arginaid: A specialized supplement containing arginine, an amino acid involved in wound healing. It's not a replacement for a complete protein shake.
🥤 Juven & Pro-Stat: Other nutritional supplements I sometimes discuss, depending on individual needs.
⚠️ One important reminder: Not every protein drink is appropriate right before surgery. Some contain added supplements, including fish oil or vitamin E, that your surgeon may want you to avoid.
Many surgical practices recommend stopping certain supplements about two weeks before surgery, so always review ingredients and follow your surgeon's specific instructions.
And remember, you don't need all these products! The goal is adequate nutrition, not buying more supplements.
Whether you're preparing for a facelift, tummy tuck, breast surgery, or another plastic surgery procedure, paying attention to your protein intake is an important part of preparation.
🤍 Save this for your surgery prep list!
Have you tried any of these protein drinks?

Day 42 Facelift Recovery - Why Am I Spitting Stitches?
I’ve been spitting stitches in my lip lift incision and possibly at the outer corners of my eyes. I started noticing small red, tender bumps forming and wondered what was going on.
It turns out this can happen when dissolvable stitches slowly work their way to the surface. It’s a common part of healing and can appear weeks or even months after surgery. Most of the time, they resolve on their own once the stitch dissolves or comes out.
