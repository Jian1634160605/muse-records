## Description
End-to-end operation of a new Instagram account for the facelift/anti-aging business: topics, scripts, AI image/video, multilingual copy with lead hooks, scheduled posting, performance reviews, and comment lead mining, with client leads as the sole success metric. Muse cannot reply to comments or DMs due to API limits; staff handle those. Videos follow phone-shot documentary realism, with AI used only for packaging and real materials required as trust anchors.
