Not wanting to make the same mistake twice is exactly the right instinct with a revision — a revision facelift isn't the same operation as a first one, because the surgeon is working through scar tissue and tissue that's already been moved once, so the consultation matters even more than the name on the door. At a revision consultation it's worth asking how often the surgeon revises other surgeons' facelifts, what they would do differently about the area that bothers you most from the first surgery, and how they judge whether the tissue can safely be lifted again. Would it help you more to know which questions separate a true revision specialist from a general facelift surgeon, or how to tell from a first consultation whether the plan fits what went wrong last time?
