Revision with thick skin is the hardest rhinoplasty category, so your instinct to research hard is right. A few things that actually separate revision specialists: first, revisions should be a large share of their practice. Primary and revision rhinoplasty are different sports. Second, for thick skin, ask to see their thick-skinned patients at 12 months or more, not 3-month photos. Swelling in thick skin takes 12 to 18 months to settle, and early photos hide the real tip definition. Third, ask how they handle tip support with rib cartilage specifically. Graft resorption and warping risk should be discussed openly, not waved away. Red flags: a surgeon who promises the simulation exactly, or steers you toward the tiny upturned nose you said you do not want. Bring your simulation to consults, but judge the surgeon on their revisions, not their renderings.
