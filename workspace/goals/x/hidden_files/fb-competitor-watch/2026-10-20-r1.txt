Good question, and an honest answer has two parts. First, timing: the neck and jawline stay swollen much longer than people expect, often 6 to 12 months before the final contour shows. Early on, edema plus a tightened platysma makes almost every jawline photograph a bit rounded, whatever the starting anatomy. Second, technique: a standardized deep-plane release does give patients a similar early signature, but original bone structure and skin thickness assert themselves as swelling resolves. The fair comparison is 12-month photos, not 3-month ones. That is when you see whose jawline is whose.
