There is no standard price because this is not one operation. Full facial rejuvenation like this is usually a combination: deep plane facelift plus neck work, often with eyelids, fat grafting, and laser. Cost follows which procedures are combined and total OR time. What actually helps at consultation: ask for an itemized quote (which procedures, whether anesthesia and facility fees are included, what the revision policy is), then compare two or three quotes line by line. Comparing headline prices without that breakdown is comparing different operations.
