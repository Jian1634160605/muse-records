DRAFT_A:
"Signing up" for a facelift usually goes like this: 1) Consultation first — in person or virtual, with clear unfiltered photos and your full medical history. 2) The surgeon should explain exactly which procedures are planned and why — a result like this is almost never one single operation (this case combined facelift + neck work + eyelids + fat transfer + laser). 3) Get an itemized quote in writing: surgeon fee, anesthesia, facility fee, and the revision policy — never book off a headline price. 4) Medical clearance happens before any surgery date is locked in. One red flag: any clinic that books you for surgery on your first visit without clearance — walk away.

## 候选 B — 顶楼回复 Brisbane 60岁 Mareea Thurn 求推荐帖（1429339452047491，Facelift Support Group）
# 帖主：60岁，Brisbane，想做 facelift + neck lift，求同龄人推荐医生。10:33 发帖，14 评论。
DRAFT_B:
Welcome to the research phase — it's the most important part. A few things worth doing before you pick a surgeon: 1) Ask to see unfiltered photos of patients close to your age, taken 12+ months after surgery — that's the real result, not the 6-week glow. 2) Ask exactly what's in the quote: a "facelift" price that doesn't specify the neck separately is a red flag — ask whether it's deep plane for face AND neck, or just one of them. 3) Ask for the revision rate and get the revision policy in writing. 4) Recovery truth: plan 2-3 weeks before you're comfortable being seen socially, 6-12 weeks for most swelling to settle, and about 6 months for the final contour. Anyone who promises "back to normal in a week" is selling, not advising. 5) Make sure it's a board-certified plastic surgeon operating in an accredited facility with dedicated anesthesia staff — non-negotiable at 60. Good luck with the journey — asking these questions already puts you ahead of most patients.
