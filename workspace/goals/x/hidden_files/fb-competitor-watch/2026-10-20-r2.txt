Two good questions, taken separately. Anesthesia: deep plane facelifts are done under general by some surgeons and under local plus IV sedation by others. Both are standard in accredited facilities, and it is a surgeon-preference thing, not a quality signal either way. What actually matters: a dedicated anesthesia provider monitoring you, and an accredited OR or hospital. Cost: the fee is mostly surgeon plus OR and anesthesia time plus facility. If budget is tight, do not pick by headline price. Get an itemized quote and compare like for like. A cheap mini lift sold at deep-plane pricing is the most expensive mistake of all.
