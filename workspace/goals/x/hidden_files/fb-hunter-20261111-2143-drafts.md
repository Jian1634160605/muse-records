## A → #530 匿名（Plastic Surgery in Turkey，posts/1783081869589931）
Good question to ask before you book — a facelift doesn't move the ears themselves, but the incision runs right in front of and behind the ear, so how the surgeon handles the tragus and the earlobe attachment decides whether the ear looks pulled or more noticeable afterwards. The two things I'd want to see in a consultation are photos of the ear area at 6+ months, not just front views, and a clear answer on whether they do any ear repositioning at the same time. Would you first ask how they protect the earlobe position, or first ask to see close-up ear photos?

## B → #519 匿名（Rhinoplasty and Revision Rhinoplasty，permalink/1137293585946982，alarplasty 怕局麻注射）
That fear is more common than people admit — the injections themselves are usually the worst part of an alarplasty under local, and the sting lasts seconds while the numbness sets in. What I'd ask at consultation is whether they offer any numbing cream beforehand, and whether the plan is local-only or local with light sedation, because those two feel very different on the day. Is it the needle sting itself that worries you most, or being awake while they work?

## C → #531 匿名（Plastic Surgery in Turkey，posts/1781965863034865，问上眼睑提升效果是否明显）
From the replies here you can see the range — for some people it's dramatic, for others subtle — and that usually comes down to what's actually making the lid look heavy. If it's loose lid skin, the change often shows once the early swelling settles over the first couple of months; if the brow has dropped as well, lid-only surgery tends to look subtle because the brow is still sitting low. The question I'd take into consultation is whether your brow or your lid is doing most of the heaviness. Would you rather hear your brow is fine for lid-only surgery, or find out the brow needs lifting first?
