## C → Lisa Queen（Truesdale Reel 1460427956042311 评论区，11-12 10:07 评论："How do you get rid of under eye darkness?"）
钩子：分型。独有细节：眼下暗沉、只问怎么去、无其他背景信息。

Under-eye darkness is one of those things with three different causes hiding under one name, and the fix depends on which one you have. If it is pigment in the skin, the answer sits with skin treatments; if it is thin skin showing the muscle and vessels underneath, the answer is usually about thickness and light; and if it is a hollow casting a shadow, the answer is about volume and contour. The fastest way to tell them apart is a consult where someone checks it in different light and with the skin gently stretched. Would you rather have a consult map which of the three is driving yours first, or start with the gentlest option for the type you suspect and see how it responds?
