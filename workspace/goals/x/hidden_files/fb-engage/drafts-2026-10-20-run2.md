---
Since you're in Brisbane doing homework — a few Australia-specific checks that matter more than before/after photos: (1) Look the surgeon up on AHPRA and confirm "specialist registration" in plastic surgery — that's the legal line in Australia between a trained plastic surgeon and a cosmetic doctor. (2) Ask for their own facelift + necklift patients at 12+ months, unfiltered — swelling can flatter results for 6-12 months, so 3-month photos flatter everyone. (3) If you travel (Turkey/Vietnam are popular for a reason), get the itemized quote and the revision policy in writing before booking flights — "we'll take care of you" isn't a policy. (4) Ask who manages you once you're home — your GP can't fix a hematoma at 2am. The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest outcome.

## T3 · 回复 "How do I sign up"

parent=2029078770966923（18:24，rc=0）
策略：中性科普"报名"=面诊流程，不提任何医生/机构

---
It usually starts with a consultation, not a booking. Most surgeons at this level do video consults: you send front/side photos in neutral light, list medications and health history, and they tell you which procedures actually fit your anatomy — a good consult narrows the plan down, it doesn't upsell. Bring three questions: what exactly will you do vs not do, can I see your patients at 12+ months unfiltered, and what's your revision policy. If the consult feels like a sales call, that's your answer.
