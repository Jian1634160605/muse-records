"How long is the recovery after a facelift?"

It's the question almost every patient asks me first. Here's the honest answer I give in consultations:

- Week 1: Swelling and bruising are at their peak. This is the real recovery phase — plan to rest.
- Week 2: Most patients return to light social life. Makeup usually covers what's left.
- Weeks 3-6: Swelling fades gradually. Some numbness or tightness is normal and resolves on its own.
- Around 3 months: The face settles. This is when I schedule the follow-up to review everything with you.

Everyone heals at their own pace — age, skin quality, and the technique all matter. That's why I map out YOUR timeline before surgery, not a generic one.
