Revision facelift: why the second lift is a different operation. 🔄

Three reasons, all at once:

1. Scar tissue. The first lift leaves scar under the skin. It hides the real tissue planes and
fights every move.
2. Skin that has already been tightened. There is less slack to work with, so the plan has to
be more precise, not more aggressive.
3. Expectations set by the first result. A revision can improve, but it cannot promise what the
first surgery didn't deliver.

That's why a good revision starts with what the first surgery left behind — not with
a photo of a face you like.
